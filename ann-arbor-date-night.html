Winter and holiday dates

1. Skate at Buhr Park

Buhr Park’s covered outdoor rink is scheduled to open Sunday, November 15, 2026 (weather permitting). Open skate is $6 for adults and rentals are $3; evening sessions run Friday and Saturday. City of Ann Arbor

2. KindleFest in Kerrytown

Friday, December 4, 2026, 5–10 p.m.: Kerrytown’s Christkindlmarkt-style holiday market, with artisan vendors, hot food, Glühwein and music from 7 p.m.; local shops stay open late. Kerrytown District

3. Hear UMS at Hill Auditorium

The University Musical Society’s season includes Handel’s Messiah (Dec. 5–6, 2026) and Yo-Yo Ma with Emanuel Ax (Dec. 10). ums.org

Arts, film and music

4. Evening at UMMA

The University of Michigan Museum of Art is always free, no tickets needed, and stays open until 8 p.m. Thursday through Sunday. umma.umich.edu

5. A movie at the Michigan or State Theatre

Marquee Arts runs both historic downtown theaters with indie films, classics and holiday favorites. marquee-arts.org

6. Folk and acoustic music at The Ark

An intimate listening room on Main Street with concerts most nights. theark.org

7. Under the dome at the planetarium

The U-M Museum of Natural History’s Planetarium & Dome Theater has shows Friday to Sunday, including Sky Tonight. Tickets are sold the day of the show. U-M Museum of Natural History

8. Author night at Literati

The downtown bookstore hosts author readings and book clubs through the season. Pick each other a book. literatibookstore.com

Free and outdoors

9. Walk Nichols Arboretum

“The Arb” is free and open daily, sunrise to sunset. Wander down to the Huron River, then get a hot drink downtown. Matthaei Botanical Gardens & Nichols Arboretum

10. Matthaei Botanical Gardens conservatory

Admission is free. The conservatory feels like summer on a grey November day. Check the conservatory hours before you go. mbgna.umich.edu

Food and play

11. Saturday at the Ann Arbor Farmers Market

The Kerrytown market runs Saturdays year-round. Pair it with breakfast or a deli sandwich nearby. City of Ann Arbor

12. Share a sandwich at Zingerman’s Deli

The Kerrytown institution on Detroit Street. Split a sandwich and browse the shelves. zingermansdeli.com

13. Play at Pinball Pete’s

Classic pinball and arcade games at 500 E. Liberty, open late. Bring a competitive streak. pinballpetes.org

Quick answers

What is a good free date night in Ann Arbor?

An evening at UMMA (free, open until 8 p.m. Thursday–Sunday), a daytime walk in Nichols Arboretum, or the free Matthaei conservatory.

What can we do on a cold winter night?

Skate at Buhr Park (scheduled from Nov. 15, 2026), catch a film at the Michigan Theater, or hear a show at The Ark.
