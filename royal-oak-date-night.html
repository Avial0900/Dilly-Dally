Winter and holiday dates

1. Wild Lights at the Detroit Zoo

The Detroit Zoo is in Royal Oak. Wild Lights runs on select nights from November 21, 2026 to January 3, 2027; gates open at 5 p.m. and it sells out, so buy tickets online ahead of time. A 21+ Igloo lounge is new this year. detroitzoo.org

2. Skate at The Rink at Royal Oak

Outdoor skating in Centennial Commons from November 20, 2026 to February 16, 2027. Skating is $10 on weekdays before 5 p.m. and $12 other times; rentals are $5. There are fire pits and marshmallow roasting. therinkatroyaloak.com

3. Royal Oak Holidays weekends

Fridays to Sundays, December 4–20, 2026: horse and wagon rides through downtown, holiday markets, a Holiday Lodge with live music, and marshmallow roasting. royaloakholidays.com

Shows and nightlife

4. Laugh at Mark Ridley’s Comedy Castle

A long-running comedy club with touring headliners most weekends (most shows are 18+). comedycastle.com

5. A concert at the Royal Oak Music Theatre

The historic theater hosts touring bands and comedians most weeks. royaloakmusictheatre.com

6. Dinner and a house beer at Lily’s

Lily’s Seafood Grill & Brewery on Washington Avenue brews its own beer and takes reservations. Dine Royal Oak

7. Stroll downtown Main Street and Washington

Downtown Royal Oak is walkable, with plenty of restaurants, bars and shops close together. Have dinner at one spot and dessert at another. therinkatroyaloak.com (downtown overview)

Games and something different

8. Solve an escape room

Breakout Escape Rooms at 222 E. 4th St. runs private, reservation-only games of about an hour. roombreakout.com

9. Bowling at Bowlero Lanes & Lounge

A 16-lane alley and lounge on Coolidge Highway, open late on weekends. bowlerodetroit.com

Daytime and free

10. See the penguins at the Detroit Zoo

In November the zoo is open 10 a.m.–4 p.m. Polk Penguin Conservation Center is a warm indoor stop on a cold day. detroitzoo.org

11. Walk Tenhave Woods

A 22-acre city nature preserve with wooded trails, free and open dawn to dusk. City of Royal Oak

12. Royal Oak Farmers Market

Saturday mornings, winter included. Pick up coffee and breakfast and plan a dinner to cook together. City of Royal Oak

Quick answers

What is a good cheap date night in Royal Oak?

Walk Tenhave Woods (free, daytime), skate at The Rink at Royal Oak ($10–$12 plus $5 rentals), or browse the Royal Oak Holidays weekends in December.

What can we do on a cold winter night?

Wild Lights at the Detroit Zoo (from Nov. 21, 2026), a show at the Comedy Castle or Royal Oak Music Theatre, or an escape room downtown.
