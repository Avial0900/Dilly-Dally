Date night ideas in Detroit

Detroit is a great city for a date: jazz clubs and a grand old theater downtown, world-class art in Midtown, and the riverfront for a walk. In November and December, the holiday rink and markets make it even easier to plan. Here are real places to try, grouped by the kind of night you want.

Winter and holiday dates

1. Skate at The Rink at Campus Martius Park

Downtown’s outdoor rink opens for the 2026–27 season on Saturday, November 21 and runs through March 7, 2027, with late hours on Fridays and Saturdays. Skate, then warm up with a drink nearby. Downtown Detroit Partnership

2. Browse the Downtown Detroit Markets

Holiday shops around Cadillac Square from November 11, 2026 to January 3, 2027, mostly Wednesday to Sunday. Easy to pair with the rink and the tree at Campus Martius. Decked Out Detroit

3. Go to Noel Night in Midtown

Saturday, December 5, 2026, 4–9 p.m.: a big holiday open-house evening across Midtown Detroit. Wayne State events

Arts, music and a show

4. Friday night at the Detroit Institute of Arts

The DIA stays open until 9 p.m. on Fridays. General admission is free for Wayne, Oakland and Macomb county residents (bring an ID with your address). Find the Diego Rivera murals together. dia.org

5. Catch a show at the Fox Theatre

The restored 1928 movie palace hosts concerts and holiday shows; December 2026 includes How the Grinch Stole Christmas! The Musical (Dec. 1–6). 313 Presents

6. Hear the Detroit Symphony Orchestra

Orchestra Hall in Midtown has classical, jazz and holiday concerts, including Home for the Holidays on December 11–13, 2026. dso.org

7. Live jazz at Cliff Bell’s

A dressed-up downtown jazz club with music most nights of the week. Book a table for dinner and a set. cliffbells.com

8. Baker’s Keyboard Lounge

A historic jazz club on Livernois Avenue with live sets. Call ahead for a table. bakerskeyboardloungedet.com

9. Bowl a few frames at the Garden Bowl

The vintage alley inside the Majestic complex on Woodward stays open late most nights. Low-key and fun for a first date. majesticdetroit.com

10. Plan ahead: Motown Museum

Hitsville U.S.A. tours reopen December 17, 2026 (tours run about an hour). Stand in Studio A where the hits were recorded. motownmuseum.org

Free and outdoors (bundle up)

11. Walk the Detroit RiverWalk

Free and open daily 6 a.m.–10 p.m. Walk the river with the Windsor skyline across the water; lovely at sunset. Detroit Riverfront Conservancy

12. Warm up in the Belle Isle Conservatory

The Albert Kahn-designed Anna Scripps Whitcomb Conservatory is free and open Wednesday–Sunday, 10 a.m.–5 p.m. Tropical plants on a cold day, then a drive around the island. Visit Detroit

Daytime dates

13. Saturday at Eastern Market

The Saturday Market runs year-round, 6 a.m.–4 p.m. Graze, people-watch and look at the murals. easternmarket.org

14. See Michigan Central Station

The restored train station in Corktown has a public lobby, and guided tours are offered. Check the visit page for current access. michigancentral.com

Quick answers

What is a good free date night in Detroit?

Walk the RiverWalk at sunset, visit the free Belle Isle Conservatory (daytime, Wed–Sun), or go to the DIA on a Friday evening if you live in Wayne, Oakland or Macomb County.

What can we do on a cold winter night?

Skate at Campus Martius (from Nov. 21, 2026), then go somewhere warm: jazz at Cliff Bell’s, bowling at the Garden Bowl, or a show at the Fox or Orchestra Hall.
